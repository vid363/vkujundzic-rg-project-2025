# vkujundzic-rg-project-2025

95/2022- Vid Kujundzic  
Simple scene in a desert with a barn, jeep with turnt lights,
ak47 resting on the barn and a helicopter above them.



## Controls

W -> Camera forwards 

A -> Camera left

S -> Camera backwards

D -> Camera right

Space -> Camera ascend

Left Shift -> Camera descend

K -> Toggle GUI

L -> Toggle flashlight
